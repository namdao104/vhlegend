Inside the rack

Water that recycles. Sensors that keep proving it.

Closed-loop liquid cooling

AI-R3 and AI-R4 connect into a closed water loop — the principle behind district cooling, scaled down to a single cabinet. Heat is recovered at the rack instead of exhausted to the room.

Retrofit programme

Can your data centre support your next generation of AI?

Four questions decide whether you retrofit or rebuild. Two or more "no" answers usually means retrofit.

01

Rack density

What is your peak load per rack today, and for your next AI cluster?

Above roughly 15 kW per rack, air alone stops being viable.

02

Cooling path

Is chilled water or a CDU loop available to the hall, or is it air only?

Liquid-ready design is expected beyond roughly 30 kW per rack.

03

Power train

Are your PDUs and busway rated for planned density, not today's?

AI racks commonly draw two to four times legacy rack loads.

04

Floor and space

Can the slab and aisle clearances take a 2.0 m, fully loaded rack?

Plan for roughly 1,200–1,500 kg per populated rack.
