Liquid cooling architecture

Chilled water runs through the rack, and recycles back.

On AI-R3 and AI-R4 the water is recycled in a closed loop, not exhausted into the room. It is the principle behind district cooling networks, scaled down to a single cabinet: heat is recovered at the rack instead of being pushed into the hall for the air handlers to fight.

Retrofit programme

Can your data centre support your next generation of AI?

Four questions decide whether a hall can be upgraded or has to be rebuilt. Two or more answers of "no" usually means retrofit rather than rebuild — and a site survey confirms the real numbers.

Rack density

What is your peak load per rack today, and what will your next AI cluster draw?

Above roughly 15 kW per rack, air alone stops being viable.

Cooling path

Is chilled water or a CDU loop available to the hall, or is it air only?

Liquid-ready design is expected beyond roughly 30 kW per rack.

Power train

Are your PDUs and busway rated for planned density, rather than today's?

AI racks commonly draw two to four times legacy rack loads.

Floor and space

Can the slab and aisle clearances take a 2.0 m, fully loaded rack?

Plan for roughly 1,200–1,500 kg per populated rack.
